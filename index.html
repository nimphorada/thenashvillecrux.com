about

The Nashville Crux is a monthly newsletter that consolidates climbing-related events that are happening in and around Nashville into one email that is sent to your inbox on the 5th of every month!

We also spotlight community members, host events, and plan outdoor trips.

So, what are you waiting for? Scan the QR code and subscribe for the next edition!
